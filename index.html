I work on embodied agents that learn long-horizon skills from offline data and world models.

I am an undergraduate student in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka. My research focuses on artificial intelligence applications in robotics, specifically investigating world models and latent space video dynamics predictors for robotic manipulation tasks.

I develop deep learning architectures using PyTorch, C++, and Python, alongside ROS2 middleware setups. My goal is to bridge offline reinforcement learning with grounded temporal representations to enable robots to operate reliably in complex environments.

RESEARCH

I want agents that can carry out long, multi-step tasks in the real world. That takes value estimates that stay accurate over long horizons, representations that capture dynamic structure, and systems that learn effectively from offline interactions.

Reinforcement Learning

Learning complex skills from offline datasets. Studying value estimation stability over long horizons under stochastic dynamics.

Embodied AI

Integrating neural perception and dynamics models for robotic controllers, focused on spatial grounding and manipulation.

World Models & Dynamics

Leveraging latent space video predictors to allow agents to simulate actions and anticipate complex environment transitions.
